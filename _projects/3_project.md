EMG signals are preprocessed to maximize signal fidelity, but such analysis is rarely conducted with participants with motor disabilities. This project examines how changing preprocessing parameters impacts signal quality and algorithm performance. We compare EMG signals from the NinaPro database, the Generic Neuromotor Interface from Meta, and data previously collected by Dr. Momona Yamagami.
